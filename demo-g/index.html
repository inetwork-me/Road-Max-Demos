FAQ

Good questions.

Can't see yours? Call 01304 350200 any time.

How quickly can you arrive?

We dispatch the nearest fitter and give you an arrival estimate when you call. Remote areas and rush-hour traffic can add a little time.

Do you offer 24-hour mobile tyre fitting?

Yes. Our service runs through the night, so an early-morning flat or a midnight puncture is handled just like a daytime call.

How do I find my tyre size?

It's printed on the sidewall, for example 205/55 R16 91V: width, profile, rim size, load index and speed rating. If you can't read it, send your registration and we'll look it up.

Do you repair punctures on the spot?

Yes. If the tyre is repairable we fix it on-site. If it isn't (sidewall damage, a blowout or low tread), we can replace it with a new or budget tyre straight away.

What affects the price?

Tyre size, brand tier, new or part-worn, tyre type (run-flat, 4×4/SUV and van tyres cost more) and the number of tyres. You'll know the full cost upfront before any work begins.
